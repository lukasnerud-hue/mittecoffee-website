About us

Good coffee,
honest work

We’ve been part of Brno’s café scene since 2012, and from day one we’ve built on quality coffee, honest work and a taste for doing things a little differently. We roast our own coffee and serve it in our cafés, alongside homemade desserts, breakfasts and everything else that belongs to a good café experience.

Mitte is a place for a morning espresso, long breakfasts, meeting friends, or moments when you just want to sit down and slow down for a while.
